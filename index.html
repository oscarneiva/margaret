Sister Margaret's Diary

In a remote monastery nestled deep within the woods, Sister Margaret spent her nights immersed in strange, arcane studies. By day, she was a model of devotion, quietly reciting her prayers, her gaze cast downward. But when the moon rose high and the chapel lay empty, she poured over ancient manuscripts and the strange devices she’d hidden away in her room.

Unknown to the other nuns, Margaret had built something extraordinary—a machine capable of interpreting divine words, a device she believed could bridge the gap between God and humankind. Yet, her discovery stirred dark suspicions, especially in Father Ignatius, the monastery’s austere and unyielding priest.

This is a portal to communicate with Sister Margaret....
